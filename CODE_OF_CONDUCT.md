# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in this project.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, and personal attacks are unacceptable.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with them.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and public interactions related to the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer through GitHub.
